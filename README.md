# Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data to identify sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, regional performance, sales team performance, sales channels, and payment methods.

The analysis was carried out using **MySQL and Microsoft Excel**.

## Tools Used

- MySQL
- Microsoft Excel
- GitHub

## Key Findings

- Total sales were approximately **₦5.02 million** across **1,000 transactions**.
- **Clothing** generated the highest sales among product categories at approximately **₦1.31 million**.
- The **North** region recorded the highest regional sales at approximately **₦1.37 million**.
- **Returning customers** generated slightly more sales than new customers.
- **David** generated the highest total sales among sales representatives at approximately **₦1.14 million**, followed by Bob at approximately **₦1.08 million**.
- **Retail** generated slightly higher total sales than Online, with approximately **₦2.56 million** compared with **₦2.46 million**.
- **Credit card** was the highest-performing payment method by total sales at approximately **₦1.76 million**.
- The highest monthly sales were recorded in **January**, while **September** recorded the lowest monthly sales.
- The average transaction value across the business was approximately **₦5,019**.

## Business Recommendations

- **Focus on the strongest product category:** Clothing generated the highest sales. The business can review the products, promotions, and customer segments contributing to this performance and consider expanding successful strategies.
- **Strengthen the North region:** The North region generated approximately ₦1.37 million in sales. The business can examine the factors contributing to this performance and apply relevant successful approaches to other regions.
- **Retain and engage returning customers:** Returning customers generated slightly more sales than new customers. Loyalty programs, personalized promotions, and follow-up campaigns could help maintain customer engagement.
- **Support high-performing sales representatives:** David and Bob each generated more than ₦1 million in sales. The business can identify effective approaches used by these representatives and share useful practices across the sales team.
- **Monitor and optimize sales channels:** Retail generated slightly higher total sales, while Online recorded a slightly higher average transaction value. Both channels should be monitored to identify opportunities for improvement.
- **Maintain convenient payment options:** Credit card generated the highest sales among payment methods. The business should continue providing popular payment options while monitoring customer payment preferences.
